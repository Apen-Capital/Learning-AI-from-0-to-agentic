# Cycle format

Each cycle covers one domain in 4 weeks and 8 meetings (16h). The theory fortnight belongs to whoever owns the domain: how to learn each subject is up to them. What the cycle fixes is the finish line of each phase. By the end of week 2 the subject is learned. By the end of week 4 there is a working artifact, presented.

## Anatomy

| Week | Phase | Monday | Wednesday |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Theory | **E1** Domain opening: the owner maps the task statements and flags the traps (a suggestion, not a rule) | **E2** Deep dive: free format (lecture, joint reading, demo) |
| 2 | Theory | **E3** Deep dive: closes the remaining task statements | **E4** Tradeoffs and case definition: the only fixed-format meeting. Discuss the domain's tradeoffs and lock the case scope before leaving |
| 3 | Practice | **E5** Set by the chosen model | **E6** Set by the chosen model: meeting, written update or returned slot |
| 4 | Practice | **E7** Set by the chosen model: rehearsal or building | **E8** Case presentation: fixed in every model. Closes the cycle |

## Practice-phase models

Four meetings in the practice fortnight are too many if the case is short and too few if the domain is new to both. So there are four models, chosen per domain at E4. E8 is always the presentation.

### A · Full pace (4 meetings, 8h)

- **E5 Kickoff:** scope, definition of done, task split
- **E6 Checkpoint:** show what broke and unblock
- **E7 Rehearsal:** run the presentation as a draft and point out holes
- **E8 Presentation**

Use when the domain is new to both and the risk of getting stuck alone is high.

### B · Build alone (2 meetings, 4h + 4h returned)

- **E5 Kickoff:** scope and definition of done
- **E6 and E7 cancelled:** the 4h go back to individual building
- **E8 Presentation**

Use when the owner is already strong in the domain and the bottleneck is keyboard time, not doubt. This model only works if the builder says so when blocked. Without that agreement, the risk is arriving at E8 with half a case and turning the presentation into a debugging session.

### C · Middle ground (3 meetings, 6h): the default

- **E5 Kickoff**
- **E6 cancelled:** 2h of building
- **E7 Checkpoint and rehearsal together**
- **E8 Presentation**

Use when there is no strong reason for A or B.

### D · Asynchronous (2 meetings + 1 written update)

- **E5 Kickoff**
- **In place of E6:** a short note in the repository: what was done, what got stuck, what comes next
- **E7 returned** to building
- **E8 Presentation**

Use when the schedule is tight. It keeps the record without spending meeting hours.

## What the presentation must deliver

E8 of every cycle. Fixed format, 2 hours.

- **The artifact running.** Not slides about the artifact: the artifact.
- **The decisions and why.** Where there was more than one path, which was chosen and what was lost on the other. This is exactly the shape of the exam questions.
- **What broke.** The error that cost the most time is usually the one that teaches the most, and it sticks until exam day.
- **The map to the task statements.** Which items of the domain the case exercised and which were left out. The ones left out become directed reading.
- **Two exam-style questions**, written by the presenter, for the other person to answer on the spot. After the five cycles that makes ten questions of our own, added to the twelve official ones.

Writing a multiple-choice question forces you to invent plausible distractors, and that is the fastest way to understand why the exam's distractor is tempting.
